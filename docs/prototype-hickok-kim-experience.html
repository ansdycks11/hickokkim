Questions we're asked most

Is the initial consultation free?

Yes. Every matter begins with a free initial consultation by phone or video, so we can understand your situation and tell you plainly whether and how we can help.

How are fees structured?

Hourly, flat-fee, or contingency depending on the matter. Personal injury cases are handled on contingency, so you pay nothing unless we recover for you. Every engagement starts with a written retainer agreement and conflict check, and invoices are itemized.

Do you meet clients in person?

Yes, by appointment. Our Los Angeles office at 2202 S. Figueroa St. is used for scheduled meetings, and we work with clients throughout California by phone and video.

Do you offer services in Korean?

Yes. Partner Daniel Kim speaks Korean and can handle consultations and matters in Korean.

What is outside general counsel?

Outside general counsel is an ongoing arrangement where the firm acts as a company's legal department without the cost of an in-house lawyer: reviewing contracts, advising on decisions, handling disputes as they arise, and keeping the company's legal house in order.

What kinds of matters do you not handle?

We do not handle criminal law. For anything else, call us: if a matter is outside what we do, we will tell you and connect you with someone who can help.
